Trippy: AI travel agent for Cleartrip

A concept that plans a work trip plus leisure days through conversation, with editable plans, budget tracking and visible AI reasoning. Built for Razorpay Design's DesignBuilder Intern assignment.

Unofficial student concept, not affiliated with Cleartrip, Flipkart or Razorpay. Prices, availability, places and reviews are simulated.
